Mission Briefing

Questions, answered straight.

The things Palm Bay homeowners actually ask before they book.

Do you offer same-day or emergency AC repair?

Yes. We provide same-day and emergency residential AC repair across Palm Bay and Brevard County, seven days a week from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Call (321) 375-5582 and we'll dispatch a licensed technician.

How often should I service my AC in Florida?

Twice a year. Florida's heat, humidity, and salt air put year-round stress on equipment, so we recommend a tune-up before cooling season and another before heating season. Both are included in every Comfort Club plan.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Launchpad Services is a fully licensed and insured Florida HVAC contractor operating under state license CAC1822716.

Do you offer financing on new systems?

Financing is available on qualifying system installations and replacements, subject to credit approval. Ask about current terms when you request a free replacement estimate.

What areas do you serve?

Palm Bay, Melbourne, West Melbourne, Viera, Rockledge, Cocoa, Cocoa Beach, Satellite Beach, Merritt Island, Indialantic, Melbourne Beach, and Sebastian — across Brevard County and the Space Coast.

What's included in a Comfort Club membership?

Scheduled seasonal tune-ups, a 21-point inspection, priority scheduling, no dispatch fees, 10% off services, 24/7 emergency response, and a lifetime warranty on covered repairs for active members. Plus and Premium add air-quality upgrades and UV protection.
