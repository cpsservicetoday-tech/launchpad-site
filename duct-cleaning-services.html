Over time, ductwork collects dust, pet dander, pollen, and debris that recirculate every time your system runs. In Florida's humidity, ducts can also harbor mold. Cleaning them improves airflow, supports better indoor air quality, and helps your system run more efficiently.

Mission Briefing

Frequently asked.

Straight answers about duct cleaning services in Palm Bay.

How often should ducts be cleaned?

It varies by home, but every few years is typical — sooner if you have pets, allergies, recent renovations, or visible buildup.

Does duct cleaning improve air quality?

It can, by removing accumulated dust, allergens, and debris that recirculate through your home, especially when paired with good filtration.

Will clean ducts lower my energy bills?

Improved airflow can help your system run more efficiently. Results depend on how restricted the ducts were to begin with.

Do you check for mold in ducts?

Yes — Florida's humidity can promote mold in ductwork. We assess for it and recommend the right solution, which may include UV systems.
