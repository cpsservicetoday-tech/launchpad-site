Mission Briefing

Frequently asked.

Straight answers about dryer vent cleaning in Palm Bay.

How often should I clean my dryer vent?

At least once a year for most homes — more often for large households or long vent runs.

Is a clogged dryer vent really a fire risk?

Yes. Lint is highly flammable, and blocked vents are a leading cause of dryer fires. Regular cleaning is an important safety step.

Will it make my dryer work better?

Yes — a clear vent lets your dryer exhaust properly, so clothes dry faster and the appliance runs cooler and lasts longer.
