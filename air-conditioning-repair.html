Repair or replace?

If your system is under ~10 years old and the repair is minor, fixing it is usually the smart call. If you're facing a major component failure on aging equipment, we'll show you the math on repair vs. a new high-efficiency system — including financing — so you can decide with full information.

What We Handle

Common AC problems we repair

Most Palm Bay no-cool calls trace back to one of these.

🌡

Warm air from vents

Refrigerant issues, a failing compressor, or airflow restrictions keeping your home from cooling.

💨

Weak airflow

Clogged filters, duct problems, a failing blower motor, or a frozen evaporator coil.

🔊

Strange noises

Grinding, rattling, buzzing, or squealing — usually loose parts or electrical faults.

♻

Constantly running

Thermostat faults, dirty coils, or low refrigerant making the system work overtime.

💧

Water leaks

Clogged drain lines or frozen coils leading to leaks and potential water damage.

⚡

High energy bills

A sudden spike often signals lost efficiency or a developing failure.

How much does AC repair cost?

My AC is blowing warm air — what's wrong?

Common causes are low refrigerant, a failing compressor or capacitor, a dirty coil, or a thermostat fault. A technician can pinpoint it quickly and safely.

Is it worth repairing an older AC?

If the unit is under ~10 years old and the fix is minor, usually yes. For major failures on older equipment, we'll compare repair vs. replacement costs so you can choose.
