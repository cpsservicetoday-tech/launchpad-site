Mission Briefing

Frequently asked.

Straight answers about emergency hvac services in Palm Bay.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

We offer same-day and emergency response 7 days a week, 7am–8pm. Comfort Club members receive priority dispatch. Call (321) 375-5582.

What counts as an HVAC emergency?

No cooling in extreme heat, no heat during a cold snap, active water leaks, electrical issues, or burning smells. When in doubt, call us.

How fast can you get here?

We prioritize emergencies and aim to respond as quickly as possible, with members moved to the front of the line during peak demand.

What should I do while I wait?

Turn the system off if you see leaking water or smell burning, keep the home shaded, stay hydrated, and check on vulnerable family members and pets.
