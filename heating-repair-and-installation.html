Mission Briefing

Frequently asked.

Straight answers about heating repair & installation in Palm Bay.

Do most Florida homes have a furnace or heat pump?

Most Brevard County homes use a heat pump, which both heats and cools. We service and install both heat pumps and furnaces.

Why won't my heat pump heat the house?

Common causes include a faulty reversing valve, low refrigerant, a failing thermostat, or a tripped backup heat strip. A technician can diagnose it quickly.

Should I get my heat checked if I barely use it?

Yes — a quick pre-season check prevents being caught without heat during a cold snap, when demand on local HVAC companies spikes.

Can you install a heat pump that also cools?

Absolutely. A heat pump is the most common and efficient choice for Florida homes, handling both heating and cooling from one system.
