Mission Briefing

Frequently asked.

Straight answers about hvac maintenance & tune-ups in Palm Bay.

How often should I get HVAC maintenance in Florida?

Twice a year — before cooling season and before heating season — because of Florida's heat, humidity, and salt air. Both visits are included in every Comfort Club plan.

Does maintenance really save money?

Yes. A maintained system runs more efficiently (up to ~30% energy savings), lasts longer, and avoids costly emergency breakdowns.

Will skipping maintenance void my warranty?

Many manufacturers require documented professional maintenance to keep warranty coverage valid. We document every visit for your records.

What's the difference between a tune-up and the Comfort Club?

A tune-up is a single visit; the Comfort Club bundles your seasonal tune-ups with priority service, fee waivers, discounts, and warranty protection for one monthly price.
