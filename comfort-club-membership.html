Important Membership Disclaimer

Lifetime warranty on repairs does not include refrigerant. Warranty coverage is only applicable while the client remains an active Comfort Club member, is responsive to scheduled maintenance, and completes a minimum of two maintenance visits per year. Warranty applies only when all technician-recommended repairs or system improvements—identified as potential contributors to future failure—are approved or addressed. All recommendations and eligibility will be clearly documented in technician service notes.

Membership terms are a binding agreement. Final plan terms, pricing, and warranty language should be reviewed by a licensed Florida attorney before publishing.

Mission Briefing

Comfort Club questions.

Everything Palm Bay homeowners ask before joining.

How much does the Comfort Club cost?

Three tiers: Comfort Club at $19.99/month, Comfort Club Plus at $39.99/month, and Comfort Club Premium at $79.99/month. All include seasonal maintenance, priority service, no dispatch fees, and a lifetime warranty on covered repairs for active members.

What does the lifetime warranty cover?

It covers qualifying repairs for active members. It excludes refrigerant, and requires you to stay current on scheduled maintenance with at least two visits per year and to address technician-recommended repairs. Full terms are documented in your service notes.

Can I cancel my membership?

Membership benefits, including warranty coverage, apply while your plan is active. Contact us at (321) 375-5582 to discuss your plan.

What's the difference between the three plans?

All three share the same core benefits. Plus adds air filters, indoor air quality improvements, and equipment safety inspections. Premium adds UV lights and elements for maximum air-quality protection.

Do members really skip the line?

Yes — members get priority scheduling and front-of-line dispatch, which matters most during summer heat waves when demand is highest.
