Mission Briefing

Frequently asked.

Straight answers about indoor air quality solutions in Palm Bay.

Why is indoor air quality worse in Florida?

High humidity encourages mold, mildew, and dust mites, and well-sealed homes trap these indoors. Targeted air-quality systems address the root causes.

Do UV lights actually help?

UV light systems neutralize mold and bacteria on the evaporator coil and in the airstream, helping keep your system cleaner and your air healthier. They're included with Comfort Club Premium.

What's the best filter for allergies?

A high-efficiency filter or electronic air cleaner captures far more allergens than a basic filter. We'll match the right option to your system and needs.

Can air quality upgrades lower humidity?

Yes — whole-home dehumidification and proper system sizing work together to control Florida's moisture, which improves comfort and protects your home.
