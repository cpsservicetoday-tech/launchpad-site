The single biggest factor in how well a new AC performs isn't the brand — it's whether it's sized and installed correctly for your home. An oversized unit short-cycles and leaves humidity behind; an undersized one runs constantly and wears out early. We start with a proper load calculation, not a guess.

Launchpad Services installs energy-efficient residential systems across Palm Bay and Brevard County, with good/better/best options so you can match the system to your budget and comfort goals. Financing is available on qualifying installs, subject to credit approval.

Mission Briefing

Frequently asked.

Straight answers about ac installation & replacement in Palm Bay.

Do you offer financing on a new AC?

Yes, financing is available on qualifying installations and replacements, subject to credit approval. We'll review current terms during your free estimate.

How long does an AC installation take?

Most residential replacements are completed in a single day. Larger projects with duct work may take longer — we'll give you a clear timeline up front.

How do I know what size system I need?

We perform a load calculation based on your home's size, layout, insulation, and other factors — never a guess. Correct sizing is the key to efficiency and comfort.

Is a new system really more efficient?

Modern high-efficiency systems can significantly lower cooling costs in Florida's long season, and proper installation maximizes those savings.
